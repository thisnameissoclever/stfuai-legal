Support

STFUAI Podcasts. A podcast player that skips the parts you did not ask for.

Email: support@stfuai.com
Community: discord.stfuai.com

Email is read by a person. Include your app version, which is at the bottom of Settings, and the name of the show or episode if the question is about one.

An advertisement was not skipped

Detection is automated, so it misses things. Open the episode, use Re-analyze, and it is examined again. You can also seek past anything by hand, and each category of skipping (advertising, self-promotion, intros and outros, closing credits) has its own switch, either for every show or for one show.

Something was skipped that should not have been

Same answer, in reverse: Re-analyze the episode, or turn off the category that caught it. The player lists every skipped segment with its type, its time range and a short summary, so you can see what it thought it was.

An analysis is not finishing

Analysis runs on our servers and each account has a weekly allowance; the plans screen shows how much of yours is left. An episode has to finish downloading before it can be analyzed. If an analysis has been queued for more than an hour, email us with the episode name.

Playback, downloads and syncing

Episodes are downloaded before they play, never streamed, so playback works with no connection once the file is on the device. Your queue and your position in each episode are stored with your account, so they follow you to another device signed in to the same account.

Managing or canceling a subscription

Subscriptions are billed by the app store you bought from, not by us, and only that store can cancel one. Open your device's app store, find its subscription settings, and cancel there. To avoid the next renewal, cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Refunds are the store's decision under the store's own policy.

Deleting the app, or deleting your account, does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in the store first.

Deleting your account

Settings has a Delete Account row while you are signed in. It removes your account, your library, your queue, your positions and the episodes you uploaded for analysis. It is immediate and it cannot be undone. What survives, and why, is set out in the Privacy Policy.

Restoring a subscription on a new device

Sign in with the same account, open the plans screen, and use Restore Purchases. That asks the store what you already own and re-grants it.
