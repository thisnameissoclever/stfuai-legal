1. What the app does

STFUAI Podcasts is a podcast player that skips parts of an episode you did not ask for: advertising, self-promotion, intros and outros, and closing credits. It downloads an episode from its publisher's public feed like any other podcast app, and when you request an analysis it sends that episode to our servers, which produce a transcript and mark the segments to skip. The skipping happens on your device during playback. We do not modify, re-host or redistribute the audio file, and the publisher still receives a normal download.
